# Cortech Consulting Group — Proposal Structure & Style Guide
*Synthesized from heading structures of 62 actual submitted proposals*

---

## 1. The Core Cortech Spine (what actually recurs)

Across the corpus, the same backbone appears again and again, regardless of numbering style. A new full proposal should contain these blocks, in this order:

1. **List of Abbreviations** — used in the longer, more polished documents (Cortech to GIZ, Cortech to DRC, Cortech to Kituo Cha Sheria, Save the Children endline, Technical Proposal 3).
2. **Cover Letter** — near-universal opener (appears in ~30 documents). Usually the first H1 unless preceded by abbreviations or a Table of Contents.
3. **Organisational Profile** — with the recurring H2s **"About Cortech Consulting Group"**, **"Our Expertise"**, and **"Our Experience"**. Sometimes framed as **"A. Consultant's Organisation"** (RACIDA, WHH, DRC, Technical Proposal 1).
4. **Related Previous Assignments** — a distinct section in one large family of documents (IOM, ZIZI Afrique, SCI, GREDO, Concern Worldwide, IGAD, ACEF, Technical Proposal 3, 4, 6). In other documents this content lives inside the Organisational Profile instead.
5. **Understanding the Terms of Reference / Understanding of the Assignment** — the most stable section in the corpus. Its recurring subsections, in typical order:
   - Project Approach and Goal / Background and Context
   - Rationale for the Assignment
   - **Our Interpretation of the Assignment** (a signature Cortech subsection — appears in at least 10 documents)
   - Purpose / Objectives
   - Scope of Work
   - Outputs and Deliverables
