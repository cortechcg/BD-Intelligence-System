# Cortech Consulting Group: EOI Structure & Style Guide

*Based on analysis of 4 submitted documents*

## 1. Core Structural Pattern (EOI Documents)

Three of the four documents are true "Expression of Interest" submissions and share a near-identical five/six-part skeleton. This is Cortech's default EOI structure:

1. **Letter of interest**
2. **Presentation of Cortech Consulting Group** (or "GROUP" — see capitalization note below)
3. **Relevant Experience of Completed Assignments**
4. **Resources in Staff**
5. *(Eligibility — optional, see variation below)*
6. **Registration and Business Information**

This pattern appears consistently in:
- *Expresion of Interest - Cortech Isebania.docx*
- *Expresion of Interest - Cortech.docx*
- *Expresion of Interest - Cortech Nandi Hills.docx*

**Guidance:** When drafting a new EOI, default to this five-heading structure (Letter → Presentation → Experience → Staff → Registration) unless the terms of reference specifically require an eligibility statement.

## 2. Honest Documentation of Variation

Do not present a single "correct" version — the real difference across the three EOIs is:

- **"Expresion of Interest - Cortech.docx"** includes an extra Heading 1 — **"Eligibility"** — positioned between "Resources in Staff" and "Registration and Business Information." This is the longest of the three EOIs (3,019 words vs. ~2,360 words for the other two), suggesting the Eligibility section was added for a bid with more stringent qualification requirements, not as a permanent fixture of the template.
- The two shorter, near-identical EOIs (Isebania, Nandi Hills) — both similar in length (2,361 and 2,355 words) — omit Eligibility entirely and go straight from Staff to Registration.

**Guidance:** Include an "Eligibility" heading only when the RFP/ToR explicitly calls for eligibility criteria to be addressed as a standalone section. Otherwise, follow the leaner 5-heading model — it's the more common pattern (2 of 3 EOIs).

## 3. The Non-EOI Outlier: UNICEF Concept Note

*UNICEF CONCEPT NOTE BY CORTECH.docx* is structurally distinct — it is **not** an EOI and should not be used as a template for future EOIs. It follows UNICEF's own concept note format, not Cortech's internal structure:

- Section 1. Concept note overview
- Section 2. Programme Strategy
- Section 3. Expected results, performance indicators, activities, implementation period and budget

Note the explicit "Section N." numbering prefix — this is unique to this document and appears driven by the funder's (UNICEF's) template requirements rather than Cortech's own convention. **Do not carry this numbering style into standard EOIs** — none of the three EOI documents use numbered Heading 1s; they use plain, unnumbered titles.

**Guidance:** If drafting a concept note or similarly funder-templated document, expect to follow the client's required section numbering instead of Cortech's standard EOI format. Section 3's heading is long and compound ("Expected results, performance indicators, activities, implementation period and budget") — this suggests Cortech follows the funder's exact heading language verbatim rather than shortening it, so preserve the funder's full phrasing when applicable.

## 4. Formatting Conventions Observed

**Numbering:**
- EOI documents: **no numbering** on Heading 1s — plain descriptive titles only.
- Concept notes (funder-driven): numbered "Section 1.", "Section 2." style — this is externally imposed, not a Cortech house style.

**Capitalization:**
- Inconsistent capitalization of "Group" in the company presentation heading:
  - "Presentation of Cortech Consulting **GROUP**" (Isebania, Nandi Hills — all-caps)
  - "Presentation of Cortech Consulting **Group**" (Cortech.docx — title case)
- This is likely an unintentional inconsistency rather than a deliberate style choice. **Recommendation:** standardize on title case ("Group") going forward for a more professional, consistent appearance, unless matching an existing template exactly.
- All other headings use consistent title case throughout all four documents (e.g., "Relevant Experience of Completed Assignments," "Registration and Business Information").

**Section naming:**
- "Letter of interest" is the only heading with sentence-case style (only first word capitalized) in the EOI documents — a minor inconsistency against the title-case convention used elsewhere. Consider whether to standardize this to "Letter of Interest" in new drafts.

**Tables:**
- No heading names (e.g., "Team," "Budget," "Key Personnel") suggest dedicated tabular sections within the Heading 1 structure itself. Tables, if used, are likely embedded *within* sections like "Resources in Staff" or "Relevant Experience of Completed Assignments" rather than called out as their own headings. Do not assume a separate "Budget" or "Team" heading is standard — it is not evidenced in these four documents.
