# Cortech Consulting Group — EOI/Proposal Style Guide
*(Synthesized from 13 submitted documents: 11 .docx with exact Heading styles, 2 .pdf with heuristically-detected headings — treat PDF-derived patterns as approximate/indicative only, since they may include cover-page fragments or table cell text mistaken for headings.)*

---

## 1. The Dominant Structure (from .docx evidence — reliable)

Two distinct document "families" emerge, used for different purposes. A new drafter should identify which family applies before writing.

### Family A: Full EOI / Technical Capability Document
Seen in *EOI Apiculture.docx*, *EOI Cortech to Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock Development (1).docx*, *EOI Cortech to Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock Development.docx*, *EOI Cortech to Ministry of Education.docx*, *EOI Cortech to SODMA.docx*. This is the house style for substantial, competitive EOIs. The consistent skeleton is:

1. **Letter of Interest** (always first, always Heading 1, always this near-exact phrase)
2. **Organisation Profile** (near-universal second heading; sometimes followed immediately by a sub-heading like "About Us," "Organisational Profile," or "Corporate Profile" — naming varies but position doesn't)
3. **Capability Statement**
4. **Resources in Staff — [Overall Organisational Organogram / Organogram and Team Composition]** (staffing visuals live here — expect an org chart or table)
5. **Technical and Managerial Capacity**
6. **Corporate/Cortech's Value Proposition** (label varies: "Corporate Value Proposition" vs. "Cortech's Distinctive Value Proposition")
7. **Our Understanding of the Assignment** (sometimes tailored: "Our Understanding of the KPEEL Assignment")
8. **Proposed Technical Approach / Proposed Implementation Approach — A [Technical] Summary**
9. **Relevant Corporate Experience**
10. *(Optional, larger bids only)* Risk Management, Quality Assurance Approach, Deliverable Alignment Matrix, Commitment to Reproducibility/Interoperability/Sustainability
11. **Why Cortech Consulting is Best Positioned for This Assignment**
12. *(Optional)* Conclusion
13. **Capability Matrix Demonstrating Compliance with the Evaluation Criteria and TOR Requirements**
14. **Eligibility** (sometimes "Eligibility and Compliance Statement")
15. **Annexes** (sometimes titled "Annex I: Registration Information" or "Registration Information" as its own heading)

**Honest variation:** Not every document includes every section. The Ministry of Education and SODMA EOIs add extra rigor sections (Risk Management, Deliverable Alignment Matrix) not present in the Apiculture or Ministry of Agriculture EOIs — these appear to scale with contract size/complexity. The shorter Ministry of Agriculture EOI drops "Conclusion" and "Proposed Technical Team" language found in the Apiculture version. Treat sections 10 and 12 (Conclusion) as optional add-ons, not core requirements.

### Family B: Short-Form EOI (small tenders/county-level bids)
Seen in *Expresion of Interest - Cortech Isebania.docx*, *Expresion of Interest - Cortech Nandi Hills.docx*, *Expresion of Interest - Cortech.docx*. Much leaner, consistent five-to-six heading structure:

1. **Letter of interest** (note: lowercase "of interest" — differs from Family A's Title Case "Letter of Interest")
2. **Presentation of Cortech Consulting Group** (or "GROUP" in all-caps in two instances)
3. **Relevant Experience of Completed Assignments**
4. **Resources in Staff**
5. *(Optional)* **Eligibility** — present only in *Expresion of Interest - Cortech.docx*, absent from the two county-specific ones
6. **Registration and Business Information**

Use this shorter pattern for smaller/local tenders where a full capability statement isn't required. Do not pad it with Family A sections unless the tender specifically calls for them.

### Family C: Financial Proposal (separate document type)
Seen in *20241108…LWF GEDSI Assessment Project.docx*. Minimal structure:
1. **Submission Letter**
2. **Financial Proposal**
3. **Fee Basis**
4. **Payment Terms**

The PDF financial proposal (*PWJ KAP Survey*) roughly corroborates this shape once cover-page noise is stripped out: Cover Letter → numbered technical sections (**FIN-3: Breakdown of Remuneration**, **Payment Milestone Schedule**, **Fee Basis**, **Payment Terms**, **Contact Us**) — suggesting financial annexes use **numbered headings** ("1 FIN-3...", "2 Payment Milestone Schedule...") even when the main EOI body does not. This numbering convention is PDF-only evidence and should be treated as approximate, but is worth adopting deliberately for financial sections since it aids navigation in cost tables.

### Family D: Concept Note (UNICEF)
A distinct, funder-mandated structure — don't generalize from it:
1. Section 1. Concept note overview
2. Section 2. Programme Strategy
3. Section 3. Expected results, performance indicators, activities, implementation period and budget

This "Section N. Title" numbering is unique to concept notes and should only be replicated when a donor template explicitly requires it.

## 2. Formatting Conventions Observed

**Capitalization:**
- Family A (full EOIs) headings are consistently **ALL CAPS** ("LETTER OF INTEREST," "CAPABILITY STATEMENT," "ELIGIBILITY").
- Family B (short-form EOIs) uses **sentence case / Title Case**, not all caps ("Letter of interest," "Presentation of Cortech Consulting GROUP" — note "GROUP" itself is capitalized inconsistently, appearing in caps within an otherwise title-case heading in 2 of 3 documents).
- Family C (financial proposals) uses **Title Case** ("Financial Proposal," "Fee Basis," "Payment Terms").
- Family D (concept note) uses **Sentence case** with numeric section labels.

→ **Guidance:** Match the ALL CAPS convention when producing a full technical EOI; switch to Title Case for short-form EOIs and financial annexes. Don't mix conventions within one document — none of the source docs do.

**Numbering:**
- Main EOI bodies (Families A, B, D) do **not** number Heading 1s in the .docx files themselves (no "1. Letter of Interest").
- Financial/annex content (per the PDF evidence) uses simple Arabic numerals prefixed to headings ("1 FIN-3...", "2 Payment Milestone Schedule," "3 Fee Basis," "4 Payment Terms," "5 Contact Us"). Annexes are also sometimes labeled "ANNEX I: ..." (SODMA EOI).
- Concept note uses "Section N." prefix.

**Tables/embedded structured content (inferred from heading names, not confirmed layout):**
- "Resources in Staff" sections consistently pair with organograms or staffing tables (explicit "Organogram" language in Ministry of Education and SODMA EOIs).
- "FIN-3: Breakdown of Remuneration" and "Payment Milestone Schedule" (PDF) strongly imply tabular cost breakdowns — line items like "Lead Consultant Person 1 30 250 7,500" appear to be table rows misread as headings by the PDF heuristic tool. **Do not treat these as literal heading text** — they are budget table rows and should be recreated as an actual table in Word, not as headings.
- "Capability Matrix Demonstrating Compliance with the Evaluation Criteria and TOR Requirements" and "Deliverable Alignment Matrix" both signal a matrix/table format is expected under that heading.

**Naming quirks to preserve or standardize (your choice, but be consistent):**
- "Organisation Profile" (British spelling) is used consistently across Family A — keep this spelling company-wide rather than switching to "Organization."
- Em-dashes appear routinely in compound headings ("Resources in Staff – Overall Organisational Organogram," "Proposed Implementation Approach – A Technical Summary"). Follow this dash style rather than colons for sub-titled headings.

## 3. Practical Drafting Checklist for a New EOI

1. **Confirm tender scale** → choose Family A (full technical EOI) or Family B (short-form) structure.
2. **Always open with "Letter of Interest"** (Family A: Title Case/caps; Family B: sentence case) — zero exceptions across all 11 docx samples.
3. **Always place "Organisation Profile"/"Presentation of Cortech Consulting Group" second.**
4. **Include "Resources in Staff" with an organogram/table**, not prose only.
5. **Close with Eligibility + Annexes/Registration Information** — this is the universal ending across every Family A and B document that includes these sections at all.
6. **Keep financial content in a separate document** (per LWF and PWJ examples) with its own light structure: Submission/Cover Letter → Financial Proposal detail → Fee Basis → Payment Terms, using numbered sub-headings and real tables for cost breakdowns.
7. **Don't invent a Conclusion, Risk Management, or Quality Assurance section unless the tender's complexity justifies it** — these are add-ons in the two largest EOIs (Ministry of Education, SODMA), not baseline requirements.

This is a multi-annex compliance-document scan, not a representative EOI narrative — use it only as evidence that **Annex packets contain registration certificates, tax clearance, and consortium/power-of-attorney documents**, not as a model for heading style or wording.
