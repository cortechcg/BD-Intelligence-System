### understanding — from EOI Cortech to SODMA.docx

Cortech understands this assignment as a strategic opportunity to strengthen the ability of Somalia's disaster-risk management architecture to convert climate and hydrometeorological information into actionable forecasts of impacts and decisions. The TOR identifies a persistent gap between the production of climate and early warning information and its translation into information about consequences for people, assets, services and priority locations. Impact-Based Forecasting provides the bridge by combining hazard forecasts with exposure, vulnerability, coping capacity and historical impact information to answer the operational questions of what may happen, where it may happen, who or what may be affected, how severe the consequences may be, and what actions should be initiated. We understand that the assignment is intentionally focused and should result in a practical and scalable national IBF framework and prototype analytical package, rather than a technologically complex national forecasting platform. The mandatory initial hazard focus is drought and flood, with demonstration and testing limited to one or two hazards and no more than two agreed test areas. The solution should maximise the use of existing meteorological, hydrological, exposure, vulnerability, food-security, population, infrastructure and disaster-loss information and should strengthen, rather than replace, Somalia's developing Multi-Hazard Early Warning System.
