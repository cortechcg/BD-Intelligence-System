## Analysis Plan

# SECTION 2 — DATA ANALYSIS PLAN

## 2.1 Quantitative Data Analysis

Quantitative data collected through structured instruments will be processed and analysed using SPSS (version 27.0 or later). Initial data management will include range checks, frequency distributions, and missing data assessment to establish data quality baselines. Descriptive statistics will characterise the study population across key demographic and programmatic variables, with results presented as frequencies, percentages, means, and standard deviations as appropriate to variable type and distribution.

Inferential analysis will employ bivariate and multivariate techniques depending on outcome variables and research questions. For continuous outcomes, independent samples t-tests and ANOVA will test differences across categorical groupings; for categorical outcomes, chi-square tests will assess associations. Regression modelling (linear or logistic as appropriate) will examine relationships between independent and dependent variables whilst controlling for potential confounders. All statistical testing will use a significance threshold of α=0.05 (two-tailed). Confidence intervals (95%) will accompany point estimates.

## 2.2 Qualitative Data Analysis

Qualitative data from key informant interviews and focus group discussions will be audio-recorded (with consent), transcribed verbatim, and imported into NVivo (version 14) for systematic coding and thematic analysis. A codebook will be developed deductively from the research framework and inductively refined during initial transcript review to capture emergent themes. All transcripts will be coded by at least two team members to establish intercoder reliability (target: Cohen's kappa ≥0.70 for core codes). Coded segments will be retrieved by theme, context, and participant type, and summarised into narrative summaries and illustrative quotes organised by research objective.

## 2.3 Triangulation and Integration

Quantitative and qualitative findings will be triangulated at two points: during analysis (to identify patterns requiring deeper exploration) and in synthesis (to construct integrated interpretation). Where quantitative results identify statistical patterns, qualitative data will explain mechanisms and contextual factors. Conversely, qualitative insights will validate or challenge quantitative conclusions. Discrepancies will be explicitly documented and analysed rather than reconciled. Integrated findings will be synthesised thematically in the final report, with each major finding supported by multiple data sources and methods.
